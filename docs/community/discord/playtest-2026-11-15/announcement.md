## Realm of Shadows — Playtest announced!
Our next playtest is planned for **Sunday, November 15 and Monday, November 16, 2026**! Start times and joining details will be announced here.

**The journey so far**
Whispering Woods (Lv. 1–10) and Crystal Caverns (Lv. 11–20), combat and classes, gathering and crafting, player housing, private chest storage, and loot/equipment displays.

**What we want finished before the test**
• **Murkmire Swamp (Lv. 21–30)** — Bog Slime, Swamp Lurker, Venom Toad, and Hydra.
• **Frostfall Tundra (Lv. 31–40)** — Frost Wolf, Ice Troll, Frozen Wraith, and Frost Giant.
• Each new island: three hunting areas and a keyed boss area, distinct platform layouts, quests, resources, and equipment.
• **Arena** — player-versus-player combat using your actual gear and trained skills.
• **Battlegrounds** — increasingly difficult monster waves, playable **solo or with a party**.
• Finish the new content, balance combat and progression, and check mobile controls, multiplayer, saving, and performance.

The new islands and modes are **planned work before the test**, not features that are already finished. The graphic shows the route we're aiming for.

Come explore, push the combat, try the systems, and help us find bugs. Your feedback will help shape the Realm!

**Save the dates: November 15–16. Watch this channel for start times and access details.**
