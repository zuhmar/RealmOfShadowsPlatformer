# Mirebound Arena

Murkmire's first area, Bog Slime Pools, has a generated crossed-swords gateway at x440 on the ground. Level 21 and normal Murkmire access are required. Walk close and interact or click the gateway. It enters a dedicated two-player room; additional challengers join another room. This first version matches by available seat, without level brackets or ranked rewards.

The arena uses the player's actual level, weapon, armor attributes, trained offensive skill and class talents. Both players press Ready; equipment, class, skin and consumable changes are blocked while ready, counting down or fighting. Cancel Ready to adjust gear. Both start at full health/mana with refreshed attack cooldowns, then have a five-second movement-locked countdown. Combat lasts up to three minutes. Existing manual movement, melee, swept projectile hits, crit, dodge, block, armor and class specials apply. Rogue follow-up strikes can be evaded. Normal mana regeneration continues; out-of-combat health regeneration waits until the duel ends. Food buffs prepared beforehand retain their normal expiry.

Knockout wins; the time limit draws. Forfeit & Leave works anywhere, and disconnect/logout immediately forfeits. The ordinary reconnect window can return the player after that forfeit, without restoring the ended match. Results remain for twelve seconds, then both must ready again. Health and mana restore after results. No equipment, gold or inventory is lost, and duels award no PvE XP, training XP, quest progress or loot. Arena status is transient, excluded from periodic/travel saves. Entry and return are free; world-map crystal teleportation cannot bypass the gateway or a duel.

The arena background and entrance are generated art, retained as original PNGs with optimized delivery copies. The arena reuses Murkmire's generated mudstone platforms and ladders at uniform scale, with a symmetric four-platform layout. The transparent HUD shows both fighters, health, countdown, timer and results on desktop and mobile.

Validation: `npm test`, `npm run typecheck`, `npm run build`, `node --env-file-if-exists=apps/server/.env --import tsx --test tests/arena.integration.ts` (local database and game server), and `node scripts/browser-arena.mjs` (local web server). Integration covers the real gateway, two-seat matchmaking, ready lock, trained damage, knockout, no PvE rewards, and saved return. Unit tests cover ranged avoidance, special attacks, timing, consent, forfeits and map reachability. Frostfall and its solo/party battlegrounds remain the next feature.
